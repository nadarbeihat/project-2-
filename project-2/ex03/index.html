opacity vs rgba

opacity: 0.5

Child

rgba(..., 0.5)

Child

opacity fades the whole element, so its text and child are faded too. rgba() is only a color value, so it fades just the background and the text and child stay solid.

Opacity cascades to all children; RGBA affects only its property.
